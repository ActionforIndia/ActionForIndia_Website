About the Cohort

AI for High-Impact Social Innovation

The AFI AI+Impact Cohort is a curated five-month accelerator program designed to support early to growth-stage startups leveraging Artificial Intelligence for high-impact social innovation across Healthcare, Agriculture, and ClimateTech. Whether you're improving healthcare outcomes, transforming agricultural productivity, or driving climate resilience through AI, this cohort is designed for changemakers like you.

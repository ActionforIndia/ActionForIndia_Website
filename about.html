About AFI

Twelve Years of Building India's Social Impact Ecosystem

Founded in 2012, Action For India is a nonprofit platform that bridges the gap between social enterprises and the resources they need to scale — capital, mentorship, technology, and networks.

Our Story

AFI was born from a simple observation: India has an extraordinary abundance of social entrepreneurs tackling the country's most pressing challenges, yet they remain isolated from the global networks, capital, and expertise that could help them scale.

By connecting India's grassroots innovators with Silicon Valley's ecosystem — its investors, technologists, and diaspora — AFI has helped hundreds of ventures grow from promising ideas to organizations serving millions.

With chapters in New Delhi, Bangalore, Silicon Valley, and London, AFI operates as a truly global platform for India-focused social impact.

Our Journey

2012

AFI founded, connecting Indian social entrepreneurs with Silicon Valley

2015

Launched first accelerator cohort; 50 social ventures supported

2017

AFI Annual Forum established as India's premier social enterprise event

2019

Crossed 500+ entrepreneurs milestone; expanded to Agriculture & Healthcare

2021

Launched Women Entrepreneurship program; ₹20M in revenue generated

2024

AI+Impact Cohort launched; 10M+ lives impacted across programs
